How it works

Three steps. No software.

1

Copy the link

Open the video on YouTube or Facebook and copy its link from the address bar or the Share menu.

2

Paste and fetch

Paste it above and press Fetch. You will see the video details and the qualities available.

3

Pick and download

Choose MP4 video or MP3 audio, press Download, and your file saves to your device.

FAQ

Good to know

Is VidNest free?

Yes. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Which links work?

Public links to single videos from YouTube (watch links, Shorts, youtu.be) and Facebook (videos, Reels, fb.watch). Playlists, live streams and private videos are not supported.

Why did my link fail?

The video may be private, age-restricted, removed, blocked in your region, longer than 60 minutes, or larger than 500 MB. Facebook videos shared only with friends can't be downloaded.

What formats and qualities do you offer?

MP4 video up to 1080p when the video has it, or MP3 audio only.

Do you keep my downloads?

No. Files are prepared on request and deleted automatically within about 10 minutes.
